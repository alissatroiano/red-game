About

Every day, a new set of letters will appear. Make as many words as you can to score high

    RULES

  • Each word MUST be at least 4 letters long
  • The letter in the center MUST be in every word you guess
  • You can use each letter more than once
  • Proper nouns, hyphenated words, and slang are not allowed
  • You CANNOT guess the same word twice

Note: Long, complex words score higher.
